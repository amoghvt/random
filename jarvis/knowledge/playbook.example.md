# Playbook (example: copy to playbook.md and make it yours)

## What I trade
- NQ, 1 contract, RTH only. Best window 9:35 to 11:30 ET. No new trades after 15:00.
- I trade reactions at: prior day high/low, overnight high/low, opening range, VWAP.
- Setup A, failed breakout: price pushes through a level, fails within 2 to 3 bars, closes back inside. Enter on the close back inside, stop beyond the failure extreme, target the opposite side of the range.
- Setup B, VWAP reclaim in a trend day: price pulls back to VWAP, holds, resumes. Stop below the pullback low.

## What I do not trade
- The first 5 minutes. The chop between 11:30 and 13:30. Ten minutes either side of CPI, FOMC, NFP.
- Anything where my stop would be more than 25 points.
- Counter-trend on a day with relative volume above 1.5 unless at an obvious extreme.

## My tells
- Two losses in a row and I start sizing up or shortening stops. The rule says stop for the day; the rule is right.
- I chase the open-drive when I missed it. If I did not have the trade planned before 9:30, I do not take it.
- I hold winners too short on trend days and too long on chop days. Trail on trend days; take the target on chop days.

## Tone I want from Jarvis
- Short. Levels, reason, invalidation. No cheerleading. If there is nothing to do, say "nothing to do".
