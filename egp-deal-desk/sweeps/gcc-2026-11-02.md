# GCC Sweep — Hunter Station
Date of sweep: 2026-11-02. Scope: UAE, KSA, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman. Mid-market situations (USD 10–150M need) with a regulator/exchange/auditor/rating signal that banks and bulge-bracket advisers tend to avoid. Every entry has at least one URL that was actually retrieved during this sweep; where a figure is derived (e.g., capital implied from loss %), that is flagged. "Approx size" figures are from the cited source or filing, not estimates.

FX used for bands: SAR 3.75 = USD 1; AED 3.6725 = USD 1; KWD 1 = USD 3.25 (approx); OMR 1 = USD 2.60; QAR 3.64 = USD 1.

## D. Regulatory position on placement/arranging — principal is a Dubai mainland LLC

1. **UAE onshore (SCA):** SCA Decision No. 13/R.M of 2021 (Rulebook) brought "arranging" and "introduction" within licensed activity; Class 5 is "advising and arranging". Promotion to Professional Investors (individuals with net assets ≥ AED 4M excl. main residence; investment companies; regulated firms; certain family offices/GREs) and genuine reverse solicitation are exempt from the local licence requirement. A mainland LLC arranging placements of securities in companies to UAE investors without a Class 5 licence relies on the Professional Investor / reverse-solicitation exemptions. Source: Clifford Chance briefing on the SCA Rulebook. https://www.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2021/07/new-sca-rulebook-cc-briefing-note.pdf ; prior framework SCA Decision 3/2017 on Promotion & Introduction (qualified-investor private-placement exemption; introductions by consultants/legal advisers exempt with commission disclosure). https://cms.law/en/are/legal-updates/promotion-and-introduction-of-financial-products-in-the-uae-the-new-framework
2. **DIFC (DFSA):** "Arranging Deals in Investments" and "Advising on Financial Products" are Category 4 Financial Services; a firm conducting them in or from the DIFC must be DFSA-authorised; base capital USD 30,000 (USD 140,000 for crowdfunding/money transmission), with expenditure-based capital typically USD 30–100k in practice. https://salvusfunds.com/2026/04/14/establishing-a-dfsa-category-3a-and-category-4-licence-in-dubai-in-2026/ ; https://neolegal.ae/insights/dfsa-category-4-advisory-arranging-licence-difc
3. **ADGM (FSRA):** "Arranging Deals in Investments" is a Regulated Activity under FSMR; FSP registers list it per firm with instrument scope and client-type limitations (e.g., Stark Securities Ltd, FSP 220140, authorised 14 Nov 2024, no retail). https://www.adgm.com/public-registers/fsra/fsf/stark-securities-limited ; FSRA Dealing & Advisory supplement: https://assets.adgm.com/download/assets/fsra-das-dealing-and-advisory-supplements-v3_0-08-20.pdf/514c3ea85b9d11eface5f2930640df3a
4. **Saudi Arabia (CMA):** "Securities business" requiring an Authorised Person licence includes "introducing parties to securities business", "advising on corporate finance", and "otherwise arranging a securities deal"; contracts arising from unlicensed securities business in the Kingdom are void; CMA publishes warnings on unauthorised persons under Article 17 CML. https://cma.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/PR/Pages/CMA-Warns-Unauthorized-APs.aspx ; https://cma.gov.sa/en/Market/NEWS/Pages/CMA_N_2772.aspx ; Amended Securities Business Regulations: https://cma.org.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Regulations/Documents/Amended%20Securities%20Business%20Regulations.pdf
5. **Practical implication for the desk:** for Saudi targets, fee-earning arranging/introduction into Saudi issuers or to Saudi investors should run through a CMA-licensed AP (e.g., the Saudi FAs listed above) or be structured as advice to the client company only, outside the Kingdom; for UAE onshore, restrict counterparties to Professional Investors and document reverse solicitation, or obtain SCA Class 5 / DFSA Cat 4 / FSRA arranging permission. This is a regulatory summary from the cited sources, not legal advice.
