# Archetype hunt — "Rescue-recap with succession" — 2026-11-06

Prepared by the Hunter station. Archetype per `playbooks/rescue-recap-with-succession.md` §1: family-controlled regulated balance-sheet business, regulatory capital shortfall from an external shock on top of under-reserving, ageing owners who have injected what they can. Excluded: everything already in `db/deals.json` (Plan Seguro, Agrocapital, Kubo, Cruz Suiza, Galeno, Cruz Blanca, Vida Tres/Banmédica, GGI, ACIG, Amana, Enaya, AMAN, Insurance House, etc.) and the candidates/watch-lists of the three 2026-11-02 sweeps (Bupa México, Prudential MX, Agropecuaria Financiera, FINDECA, Social San Juan, Caja Maynas, ANS Jan–Jun 2026 cohort, Proaktiva, FINACTIV, Agrofinanciera, UCAY, ASP, Quálitas, Centauro, Credivalores, Shuaa, Ithmaar, Takaful Emarat, GVO Oldenburg).

## Method

- 52 WebSearch queries (extended mode for registers; Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Polish, Greek, Romanian, Indonesian, German) across (1) Mexico CNSF/CNBV/raters, (2) rest of LatAm (SSN, SFC, SBS, SUSEP/ANS, CMF, Central America/DR/Panama), (3) GCC (Saudi IA/CMA, CBUAE, Oman/Bahrain/Kuwait/Qatar), (4) Southern/Eastern EU NCAs (DGSFP, IVASS, ASF PT, BoG, KNF, ASF RO, FSC BG, HANFA, Cyprus, Malta) and (5) SE Asia (OJK, Philippines IC, Vietnam MoF, Thailand OIC).
- WebFetch of the source page for every candidate kept; where the page was a PDF the text was extracted locally (BMV rating event 09/03/2026; Moody's Local 11/03/2025; General de Salud RSCF 2025; CNSF open-data sanctions CSV).
- §4.3 negative screens applied explicitly: administrator/liquidator appointed → out; licence revoked → out; auditor disclaimer / fraud indicators → flagged; solvency < 0.8x where known → out.
- All amounts in local currency unless stated; USD conversions are approximate (MXN 18.5, ARS 1,450, SAR 3.75, IDR 16,500, BRL 5.4) and are mine.
- Figures and names are only those on retrieved pages; anything else is marked "unverified".

Honest headline: the hunt produced **9 candidates that survive the screens** (3 with archetype fit ≥ 3/5), **4 cohort clusters** worth working through intermediaries, and a **watch-list of 8**. Southern/Eastern EU and Oman/Bahrain/Kuwait/Qatar produced nothing that fits; Mexican ISES capital trouble is still not visible in any public register (see Cohorts §1 for the proof).
