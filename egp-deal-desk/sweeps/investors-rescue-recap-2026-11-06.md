# Investor Graph v1 — Rescue-recap with succession (regulated financials)

Prepared 2026-11-06 by the Investor station. Scope: investors that actually closed (or signed) primary recapitalisations, minority or control investments in family-owned / mid-sized regulated financial businesses (insurers, health insurers, takaful, non-bank lenders, consumer finance, broker-dealers, regional banks) in LatAm (Mexico first), GCC, Southern/Eastern Europe and SE Asia, 2022–2026, plus the DFIs and reinsurer arms of playbook §6.1.

Machine-readable twin: `db/investor-candidates.json` (35 records, status `candidate`). `db/investors.json` (Amogh's own network) untouched.

## Method

- 41 web searches (mostly extended) and 20 page fetches, 2026-11-06. Query families: regulator change-of-control notices (CNSF/SHCP, SBS, SFC, CMF, SUSEP, Saudi IA/CMA, CBUAE, OJK, Insurance Commission PH, BNM, EIOPA/NCA), DFI disclosure (IFC, IDB Invest, FMO, BII, Proparco, DEG, EBRD), deal press in ES/EN/PT, run-off specialists, reinsurer strategic arms, family offices that own insurers.
- One structured source was decisive: Milliman, *Life and health insurance M&A in 2025 and beyond* (Apr 2026, S&P data) — fetched as PDF and text-extracted. It confirms LatAm life/health M&A collapsed to **one** announced deal in 2025 (Consorcio Financiero buy-out of IFC, Chile, USD 96M) and lists Nortia/Expertia as the only Spanish family-office insurer stake of 2025. https://media.milliman.com/v1/media/edge/images/millimaninc5660-milliman6442-prod27d5-0001/media/Milliman/PDFs/2026-Articles/4-7-26_2026-Milliman-MA-Report.pdf
- Rules applied: every investor has at least one retrieved URL per evidence deal; where a deal is outside 2022–26 it is labelled; amounts are "unverified" when no source gives them; no emails; contact path is a retrieved website URL or "unverified". Regulator registers themselves were mostly not reachable through search (see Gaps) — the approval evidence below comes from press releases and filings that cite the approvals.
- Fit score (1–5) = (a) prior regulated-financial primary/minority deal, (b) geography, (c) ticket USD 10–80M, (d) evidence of accepting minority with governance, (e) approval experience.

Counts: financials-specialist PE 8 · special situations 3 · strategic insurer/bank 12 · family office 4 · reinsurer strategic arm 1 · DFI 5 · local institutional 2 = **35**.
