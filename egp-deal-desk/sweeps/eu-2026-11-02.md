# EU Sweep — Hunter station — 2 Nov 2026

Scope: European mid-market (DACH, Benelux, Iberia, Nordics, Italy, PL/CEE, UK) showing recap / refinancing / rescue-equity / succession signals, EUR 10M–100M ticket, pre-insolvency or self-administration stage. Every entry below is backed by at least one URL actually retrieved on 2 Nov 2026. Figures marked "(uncertain)" were not confirmed in a retrieved source. Companies already in liquidation (e.g. Accell Group, Sowitec group, Schlote subsidiaries, Eitek — assets sold to Kaitec) were screened out.

Method note: TMA Deutschland news feed and 9fin/Debtwire aggregate pages named no specific debtors for 2026; the candidates below came from company ad-hoc releases, trade press (INDat, GÖRG/Chambers releases, Möbelmarkt, WiWo, Leadersnet/KSV1870, AKV, Milano Finanza, Collettiva, AeroTime, Democrata, El Cronista) and regulator pages (BaFin measures list, GVO annual report).
