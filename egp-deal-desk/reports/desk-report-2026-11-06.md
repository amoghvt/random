## The one thing to read

**Re-verification cut every score from last week's sweep.** Seven Priority targets went in at 71–80; they came out at 43–71. Three survive as Priority (Schüle 70, Windmöller 71, Takween 70), three drop to Watch (Eliance 66, Agrocapital 66, Gilmar 62), one parks (Cruz Suiza 43: its ARS 48.6M deficit, about USD 35k, was paid by the owners in May and the inhibition lifted in June; the sweep missed the cure).

That is the desk working as designed, and it carries a lesson. Last week's Hunter found **insolvency-process situations** (Schutzschirm, preconcurso, composizione negoziata). Those are visible precisely because they are already someone else's process: by the time we read about them an M&A adviser is being picked (Schüle), the CRO's own firm sells financing advice (Windmöller), the underwriter is appointed (Takween), the owner has stopped funding (Eliance), or the plan is an asset sale (Gilmar). The desk's real edge is the archetype in the playbook: a **regulated financial, family-owned, below its capital floor, with a succession problem**, where nobody is running a process because nothing has been filed. Those are invisible in registers and visible only through rating-agency text, solvency reports and intermediaries. This week's archetype hunt proved it (see Cohorts).

## Archetype hunt (new, 6 Nov) — what it found and what it proved

Nine candidates survive the playbook's negative screens; none is Priority yet. Loaded as `sourced` with a 13 Nov next action each. Full entries with URLs: `sweeps/archetype-rescue-recap-2026-11-06.md`.

1. **La Latinoamericana Seguros (MX), score 56, fit 4/5.** The only true pattern match: 120-year family insurer (chairman Rodrigo Amerlinck), family CEO replaced by a professional Feb 2025, PCR Verum note of 9 Mar 2026 shows minimum-capital coverage 1.07x and a 2025 loss, VAT cost arriving in 2026. Plan Seguro's pattern one size down, same city. Path: PCR Verum / AM Best Mexico, then a direct letter to the chairman citing the rating note.
2. **Al-Etihad Cooperative Insurance (KSA), score 67.** Hardest signal of the week: solvency below the IA minimum, accumulated losses 50.4%, going-concern note (5 Nov), EGM pending. Anchor is Al Ahleia (Kuwait), not a family; fits as a GGI-style placement with a GCC investor, USD 40–80M. Path: Lux Actuaries, Albilad.
3. **Select Operadora de Plano de Saúde (BR), 51.** Private health-plan operator under ANS direção fiscal since 18 Sep 2026; the one non-cooperative in the Brazilian cohort where equity is possible; owners unknown.
4. **Victoria Insurance (ID), 43.** Rp250bn deadline 31 Dec 2026; the Rp1tn 2028 rule makes Indonesia a 2027 pipeline, not a 2026 one. Build the Jakarta intermediary map now.
5. **Libra Seguros (AR), 56 but flagged.** Single owner 83%, litigated-claims reserve hole, auditor disclaimer. Roll-up anchor only.

**What it proved.** The CNSF open-data sanctions file (updated 30 Sep 2026) holds only MXN 20k–260k reporting fines, published 12–18 months late; Plan Seguro's own June 2026 Art. 321 regularisation plan is absent. Mexican capital measures are therefore visible only in (a) rating-agency text, (b) the RSCF solvency reports each May, (c) intermediaries. Southern/Eastern EU produced nothing: sector solvency ratios are 167–273% and news search is the wrong tool; the ~150 family-owned non-life insurers' SFCRs need scraping each April–May for ratios below 130%.

**Cohorts to work (in order).** Mexico VAT (via PCR Verum, HR Ratings, Moody's Local México, AM Best Mexico, AMIS, second-tier auditors, reinsurers' Mexico cedant desks at 1 Jan renewals) · Saudi IA SAR 300M minimum + RBC Jan 2027 + CMA ≥50%-loss regime (via Lux Actuaries, Albilad, A&O Shearman, Jawlah feed) · UAE six insurers below solvency, names held by Lux (one meeting in Dubai) · Indonesia POJK 23/2023 Stage II (2028) · Argentina SSN floor as a roll-up only · Brazil ANS direção fiscal via Demarest.
