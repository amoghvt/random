**Regulatory flag — Saudi (targets 3, 6, 8):** CMA treats "introducing", "advising on corporate finance" and "arranging a securities deal" as licensable securities business, and contracts from unlicensed activity in the Kingdom are void. Any fee-earning role must run through a CMA-licensed Authorised Person (Albilad, Wasatah, Estedemah, SNB Capital, GIB Capital are all active on these exact deals) or be structured as advice to the issuer delivered outside the Kingdom. Co-investment through EGP's own investors into a licensed process is the cleanest route. Local counsel before any engagement letter.

**UAE onshore:** restrict to Professional Investors and document reverse solicitation, or obtain SCA Class 5 / DFSA Cat 4 (base capital USD 30k). Worth pricing a DFSA Cat 4 licence if the GCC pipeline proves real.
