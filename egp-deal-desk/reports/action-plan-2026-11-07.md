1. You approve a message in the dashboard (Outbox → Approve to send, with a note if you want a change) or by name in chat.
2. The Operator sends it **from your Gmail account** through the Gmail connector, as drafted plus your note. Nothing else is changed; a change of recipient or substance goes back to the outbox for a new approval.
3. The message moves to `sent/`, the record moves to `approached`, a follow-up date is set at +7 days, and the dashboard is republished.
4. Replies are read from your inbox daily; each one gets a drafted response in the outbox, and silence gets a follow-up draft at +7 and +14 days. After three silences the record goes dead with a re-sweep date.
5. From the first reply onward the Operator drives the stages: call brief → readiness proposal + NDA → first-10-days request → engagement letter → investor process → term sheet. Every outbound at every stage is a draft, an approval, a send.
