**Provider:** Enterprise Growth Partners LLC, Dubai

**Work / readiness fee:** EUR <25,000–60,000>, payable on signature, non-refundable, not credited against any other fee. Covers restatement, data room, regulatory memo, investor memorandum, NDA.
**Success fee:** <5–7>% of capital raised from investors introduced by EGP, "or such greater amount as is actually received", payable within 10 business days of EACH tranche received by the client (tranches named explicitly: convertible loan on signing, balance on regulatory approval).

**Expenses:** pre-approved, reimbursed. **Information:** client responsible for accuracy; mutual indemnity; EGP is an adviser, not a licensed placement agent; client confirms local securities-law compliance.
