# Data room index — standard structure with gap list

Folder numbering is fixed so investors can compare deals. Each folder carries a gap list (document, owner, due date). The index itself is shareable before the NDA; contents only after.

0. Snapshot — the frozen one-paragraph story; amount, stake, look-through, primary/secondary, solvency figure. Must match the deck and every email (`tools/consistency_check.py`).
1. Corporate — registry extracts (holdco and opco, with numbers), ownership chain to UBOs, pledges, shareholder agreements, board minutes 24 months.
2. Financial — audited statements 5 years, interims, management accounts, restatement workbook (incurred vs booked vs paid), normalised earnings bridge.
3. Regulatory — licences, solvency reports, regulator correspondence 24 months, plans filed, regulatory counsel's opinion on open proceedings.
4. Actuarial / asset quality — reserve methodology, appointed actuary's reports, independent review scope and (when ready) opinion, reinsurance treaties.
5. Tax — position memo, retroactive items, settlements.
6. Commercial — distribution (brokers/channels), top counterparties, contracts, pricing plan for next renewal cycle, portfolio exits.
7. Legal — litigation, supplier disputes, regulatory sanctions, material contracts.
8. HR — organisation, key-person contracts, succession.
9. Transaction — indicative term sheet, sources and uses, dilution tables, approval timeline, investor Q&A pack.
