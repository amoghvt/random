# Recapitalisation Readiness Programme — proposal outline

(Operator drafts from this; Amogh's existing Biltmore readiness agreement is the legal base — this is the commercial outline only. Not legal advice; local counsel reviews.)

**Client:** <holdco/company>  **Provider:** Enterprise Growth Partners LLC, Dubai
**Term:** 30 days from signature (extendable by agreement)
**Fee:** EUR <25,000–35,000>, payable on signature. No success fee on this programme. Fee is not credited against any subsequent mandate.

**Scope / deliverables**
1. Financial restatement: management accounts and statutory statements (last 3–5 years) reconciled; off-balance-sheet and contingent liabilities identified and scheduled; normalised EBITDA / solvency position.
2. Data room: structured index (corporate, financial, regulatory, legal, commercial, HR); gap list with owners and dates.
3. Investor memorandum: 15–25 pages, investor-grade; sources and uses; transaction structure options (primary recap / holdco loan / secondary) with dilution tables.
4. Diligence Q&A pack: anticipated investor questions with draft answers and references.
5. Transaction structure recommendation and timeline.

**Client obligations:** full information access; one executive point of contact; responses within 3 business days.
**Confidentiality:** mutual NDA; EGP may not disclose client identity to investors during this programme.
**Governing law / disputes:** DIFC law, DIAC arbitration (EGP standard) unless client jurisdiction requires otherwise.
**Next step:** on completion, Capital Advisory Mandate (separate agreement) for the raise.
