# Investor first message — template and rules

Rules: ≤ 150 words. To a qualified investor only (the five questions answered). The bad number is in the first paragraph. No adjectives about the company. No investor names of others. Signed by Amogh. Sent only after Amogh approves the draft in `outbox/`.

```
Subject: <Country> <sector> — primary recap at restated book, <USD band>

<Name>,

You said you would look at a regulated-financial primary raise if the reserving was shown up front. This one: a <country> <sector> with <revenue/premium band>, <N> years old, family-owned. A <tax change / claims shock> took it below the regulator's capital floor; on an incurred basis the combined ratio is <x>% against the booked <y>%, and <advances / other debtors> stand at <z>x equity. The owners have injected what they can and take no proceeds.

Structure: new shares in the holdco for <stake>% (look-through <n>% of the opco), priced on restated book with a price adjustment to an independent actuarial review; tranche 1 as a convertible loan on signing, balance on regulatory approval. Return is margin repair and re-rating, not growth; exit to a strategic that wants the licence.

Twenty minutes to walk you through the restatement? The data room index is attached.

Amogh Tiwari
Enterprise Growth Partners, Dubai
```

Variants:
- **Strategic insurer / bank:** lead with the licence and distribution ("250 hospitals on direct pay; 95% broker-distributed"), the control path after year 4, and the regulator's familiarity with the business.
- **DFI:** lead with access (members, SMEs, regional coverage), governance (board seats, reserved matters) and the independent review already scoped; expect 6–12 months and say we know it.
- **Reinsurer strategic arm:** lead with the capital-relief structure (quota share / LPT) alongside the equity; the equity ticket is the smaller part.
