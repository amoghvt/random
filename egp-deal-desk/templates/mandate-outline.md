# Capital Advisory and Investor Introduction Agreement — commercial outline

(EGP's signed Revolo/Biltmore agreements are the legal base. This is the term summary the Operator uses to draft proposals. Not legal advice.)

**Parties:** Enterprise Growth Partners LLC (Dubai) · <Client>
**Purpose:** advise on and introduce investors for a raise of <target> via <structure>
**Term:** 12 months; tail 18–24 months on investors introduced by EGP
**Exclusivity:** limited to EGP's investor territory (family offices / HNWIs / private groups in EU, UK, US, Australia, GCC); client may run other advisers elsewhere
**Engagement fee:** EUR 25,000 on signature — consideration for Mandate Establishment Services (onboarding, KYC, investor packs tailored per investor, meetings). Not creditable against success fee. (Waivable only for exceptional assets, in exchange for a higher success fee and equity.)
**Success fee:** 5–7% of capital raised from investors introduced by EGP, payable at each closing; 7% for distressed/recap situations, 5% for clean growth raises
**Equity / warrants:** 1–3% of the holdco (or warrants at the round price), vesting on closing — asked on every mandate
**Expenses:** pre-approved travel reimbursed
**Warranties & indemnity:** client responsible for accuracy of information; mutual indemnity; EGP not a broker-dealer/licensed placement agent — advisory role; client confirms the raise complies with local securities law
**Governing law / disputes:** DIFC, DIAC arbitration
**Regulatory flag for the draft:** confirm per jurisdiction how introduction activity is treated (UK FCA arranging; US broker-dealer; EU MiFID; DFSA/FSRA if the client sits in a UAE free zone; Saudi CMA). Structure as advisory-with-success-fee where needed; local counsel to confirm.
