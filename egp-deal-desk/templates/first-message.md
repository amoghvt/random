# First message — template and rules

Rules: ≤ 120 words. No greeting filler. No deck attached. One public signal, one comparable, one ask. Signed by Amogh, EGP.

```
Subject: <Company> — <the specific situation, 4 words>

<Name>,

I read the <CNSF notice / Fitch action / StaRUG filing> on <Company> from <date>. The <capital requirement / maturity / claims impact> looks like roughly <band> that the banks won't lead on while <the complication> is open.

We just did exactly this for a <country> <sector> company: restated the financials, built the data room and structured a USD 22M recapitalisation with a single investor from our network, inside 60 days, after their banks stepped back.

If useful, I'd take 20 minutes this week to show you how it was structured. No deck, no pitch — just the mechanics.

Amogh Tiwari
Enterprise Growth Partners, Dubai
```

Variants:
- **Via intermediary** (to the lawyer/auditor): "You'll see more of these than I do. We pay a referral fee on engagements and we make your client's process shorter. Worth a coffee?"
- **Succession/family**: lead with "partial sale without losing control" rather than distress.
- **Biotech/growth**: lead with investor type ("family offices in the EU/GCC active in <indication>") and the 5% success-only option if the asset is exceptional.
