# First-10-days information request (playbook §7)

Sent to management on signature of the readiness agreement, in this order. One executive point of contact; answers within 3 business days per item.

1. Audited financial statements, last 5 years, with notes; latest interim; solvency reports; the regulator's own control figures.
2. All regulator correspondence in the last 24 months: notices, plans filed, sanction proceedings, acknowledgements.
3. Claims / loans on an incurred vs booked vs paid basis, 3 years + interim: the reconciliation that reveals the true loss ratio.
4. Counterparty payables and "advances" / "other debtors" by name, with ageing; any supplier suspensions or litigation.
5. Reserve methodology filings and the appointed actuary's reports.
6. Reinsurance / funding treaties and their reconciliation.
7. Tax position: retroactive changes, settlement schedules, counsel memos.
8. Ownership chain with UBOs, pledges, shareholder loans, capital injections in the last 24 months and their source.
9. Management projection with pricing and portfolio-exit detail; sales plan for the next renewal cycle.
10. Dividend history (statement of changes in equity), including anything paid during the distress period.

Compute before any investor sees a page: incurred-basis loss and combined ratios; restated equity; capital need on three bases (management's; restated to 1.0x; restated to 1.3x with headroom); look-through ownership of the ticket; pre/post-money of the issuing entity; normalised earnings after clean-up and re-rated value at 8–10x; value of one combined-ratio point in local currency and USD.

Red-flag tests (stop and escalate if any fail): solvency held only by month-end payment deferrals; dividends paid while shareholders inject; appointed actuary will not certify "no unrecognised loss"; regulator silence presented as approval; owner wants to hide the raise from management or vice versa; capital instalments to the regulator missed.
