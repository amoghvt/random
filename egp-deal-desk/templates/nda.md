# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — commercial outline

(Operator drafts from this once a target agrees to a first call with documents. Commercial outline only, not legal advice; local counsel reviews before signature. EGP's signed Biltmore NDA is the legal base.)

**Parties:** Enterprise Growth Partners LLC (Dubai) · <Company / holdco>
**Purpose:** evaluation of a possible readiness programme and capital advisory mandate ("the Purpose")
**Confidential Information:** all non-public business, financial, regulatory and legal information exchanged for the Purpose, in any form, including the fact that discussions are taking place
**Carve-outs:** public domain (not through breach); already held; independently developed; received from a third party without restriction; required by law or regulator (notify first where lawful)
**Permitted disclosure by EGP:** to its advisers and, after the client's written consent per investor, to named prospective investors who have signed an NDA no less protective — EGP never names investors to the client before this NDA, and never names the client to investors during the readiness programme
**Term:** 2 years from signature; obligations on regulated / personal data survive as required by law
**No obligation to transact; no licence; no warranty on information** (client responsible for accuracy under the later engagement letters)
**Return / destruction:** on request, with one archival copy permitted for compliance
**Non-circumvention (optional, ask every time):** client will not approach investors introduced by EGP for 18 months other than through EGP
**Governing law / disputes:** DIFC law, DIAC arbitration (EGP standard) unless the client's jurisdiction requires otherwise
