## Angle
We read the FY2025 accounts note that Eliance needs its sole shareholder's support, then the 8 Oct preconcurso and the 5 Nov grounding of both Madrid SUMMA 112 aircraft with daily fines; the EUR 15–40M that fixes training, maintenance and the Infranity facility is money no bank leads on with a CNMC probe and a court process open; we did exactly this for a Mexican health insurer (USD 22M from one investor, financials restated, data room, inside 60 days after the banks stepped back); 20 minutes to show the mechanics.
Front door: Readiness Programme (EUR 35k, 30 days) — restated numbers, contract-by-contract viability map, data room, transaction structure (new money vs. unit sale). Low risk for the owner; produces the material either for new money or for a clean unit sale. If a concurso is confirmed, the front door becomes "investor sourcing for the unit sale" on success fee, with a reduced readiness fee for data-room build.
Behind the door: Capital Advisory Mandate (EUR 25k + 6% success) + 2% warrants in the recapitalised holdco (expect to lose the equity ask against an institutional owner; ask anyway).

## Fee design
Readiness: EUR 35k on signature.  Engagement: EUR 25k.  Success: 6% of new money raised or unit-sale proceeds from EGP-introduced investors.  Equity/warrants: 2% of holdco (negotiable to zero).
Rationale: ticket is mid-band (EUR 15–40M) and urgent (3-month preconcurso window or an already-running concurso), so success fee sits mid-range at 6%; competition is real (Lazard/big-four relationships of the owner) so the readiness fee stays at the top of range only because it is owner-funded and buys a 30-day product the owner needs anyway for any exit. Potential fee: (35k + 25k) EUR = EUR 60k ≈ USD 70k; 6% × EUR 27.5M mid-point = EUR 1.65M ≈ USD 1.91M; total ≈ USD 1.98M at EUR/USD 1.16 (assumed).
