# Call brief — Agrocapital del Noroeste (Grupo Ceres) — first call, date TBC (prepared 2026-11-06)

**Who's on the call:** Best case Ing. Guillermo Elizondo Collard, founder and Presidente del Consejo of the Grupo Ceres companies (https://grupoceres.com.mx/fundador.html; born 1931) — decides everything, afraid of losing control of a 65-year family franchise and of the Los Mochis market learning Agrocapital is in trouble (sourced guess: he cancelled Expoceres 2025 publicly rather than hide the crisis, https://www.luznoticias.mx/2024-09-18/agro/se-cancela-expo-ceres-2025-guillermo-elizondo/218523). More likely: the group's finance lead or Agrocapital's director general — names unverified — who decides nothing alone and is afraid of the funder (development bank, likely FIRA, unverified) calling the MXN 880.6M line and of a second PCR Verum cut.
**Our one line:** PCR Verum's 23 Jul 2026 cut to B/M with the single line restricted means either USD 10–15M of capital the funder will accept, or run-off; we closed a USD 22M single-investor recap for a Mexican insurer inside 60 days after its banks stepped back, and we'd show the mechanics in 20 minutes.
**What we must learn (in 20 min):**
1. Real number: who is the funder (FIRA? which programme?), what exactly has it said — standstill, reduce-to-zero schedule, demand? What are the 2Q26 and 3Q26 cartera vencida, reserves, cash and equity? How much capital would the funder need to see to re-open the line, and by when? Any PCR Verum deadline communicated to them?
2. Who decides: does the founder sign, or a group CEO/CFO? Is the family aligned on keeping Agrocapital vs running it off? Who is their lawyer and auditor (we need the names for the data room and as future intermediaries)?
3. What's been tried: have they approached another development bank, a commercial bank, a portfolio buyer, a local boutique? Did Grupo Ceres consider injecting capital itself? Why did it stall?
4. Can they fund a EUR 25k readiness fee this month, from the group if not from the SOFOM?
5. Off-book: how much of the book is lent to Grupo Ceres' own customers (seed, fertiliser, machinery buyers) or affiliates? How much is restructured (14.8% in 2025 per PCR Verum)? Any guarantees given by the group to the funder? Ask directly; the hesitation is the answer.
**What they'll object to:** fee up front → "covers real work (loan-tape restatement, data room, instrument), signals seriousness, not credited"; exclusivity → "limited to our investor territory; your bank and FIRA relationships stay yours"; "FIRA will sort it out / we have a bank" → "a development bank cannot put equity in; it will re-open the line only against capital someone else provides — we bring the people who price a 13% NPL book"; "we don't want outside shareholders" → "sub-debt or preferred, control stays with the family; the instrument is designed for the funder".
**What we never say:** guaranteed raise, timing promises, the funder's likely reaction, investor names before NDA, legal or tax opinions (withholding, LMV), anything about the other two agri SOFOMs in our pipeline.
**Close:** propose the Readiness Programme at EUR 25k, 30 days, deliverable: restated book + data room + memorandum + a run-off-vs-recap comparison they can take to the funder; send the one-page outline the same day.
