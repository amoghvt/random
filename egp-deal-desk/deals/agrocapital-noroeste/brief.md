# Agrocapital del Noroeste, S.A. de C.V., SOFOM E.N.R. — Mexico (Los Mochis, Sinaloa), agri non-bank lender              Score: 66  [Watch]
As of: 2026-11-06

**What they are.** Agricultural non-bank lender (avío, refaccionario, empresarial, prendario, operating leases) founded June 2005 in Los Mochis as a subsidiary of Grupo Ceres S.A. de C.V.; loan book MXN 1,019.7M (~USD 55M at ~18.5), equity MXN 214.0M (~USD 11.6M) at 1Q26; Sinaloa ~57–65% of the book, expansion into Bajío (PCR Verum, 23 Jul 2026, https://pcrverum.mx/calificaciones/pcr-verum-baja-calificaciones-de-agrocapital-a-b-m-y-4-m-y-las-coloca-en-observacion-negativa/ ; PCR Verum, 31 Jul 2025, https://pcrverum.mx/calificaciones/pcr-verum-afirma-calificaciones-de-bbb-m-y-3-m-de-agrocapital/ ; 31 Jul 2024, https://pcrverum.mx/calificaciones/pcr-verum-ratifica-calificaciones-de-bbb-m-y-3-m-de-agrocapital-revisa-perspectiva-a-estable/). Grupo Ceres is a private agribusiness group (seeds/fertiliser Seferssa, John Deere dealer Agroequipos, warehousing Armex, grain trading Mercanta, Topolobampo port terminal, agri insurer Asterra, Inmobiliaria Ceres) founded 1959–60 by Ing. Guillermo Elizondo Collard, "Fundador y actual Presidente del Consejo de Administración", born 15 Aug 1931 — i.e. 95 years old (grupoceres.com.mx, https://grupoceres.com.mx/ and https://grupoceres.com.mx/fundador.html ; Luz Noticias, 18 Sep 2024, https://www.luznoticias.mx/2024-09-18/agro/se-cancela-expo-ceres-2025-guillermo-elizondo/218523). No other shareholder, successor or Agrocapital executive is named in any retrieved source — **unverified**.

**What's wrong.**
- 23 Jul 2026 — PCR Verum cut Agrocapital to B/M from BBB/M and 4/M from 3/M, both on Observación Negativa, citing deterioration of asset quality: cartera vencida MXN 134.8M = 13.2% (13.8% adjusted for write-offs) vs MXN 57.1M (5.6%) at 1Q25; reserves MXN 38.5M = 0.3x coverage (0.6x a year earlier); cash MXN 1.5M = 0.2% of interest-bearing liabilities; foreclosed assets MXN 35.9M; low-productivity assets 79.1% of equity; net income MXN 4.4M for 3M26 (ROAE 8.3%). All bank/institutional borrowings (MXN 880.6M, ~USD 48M) sit with ONE funder, whose minimum NPL covenant was breached, so the main line is restricted (no new origination). The agency says a further cut "podría materializarse durante los próximos meses" — no hard date (URL above).
- The single funder is described in the 2024 and 2025 releases as "una institución de Banca de Desarrollo" (80.5% of funding 2Q24; 82.8% of MXN 1,690M of lines 1Q25), not named. FND was wound up in 2023 (Luz Noticias, 1 Jun 2026, https://www.luznoticias.mx/2026-06-01/agro/el-gobierno-de-mexico-condonara-deudas-a-productores-de-sinaloa-/291404), and Agrocapital was a FIRA/PROIF dispersing intermediary in 2023 (Tus Buenas Noticias, 16 Feb 2023, https://www.tusbuenasnoticias.com/noticias/sinaloa/2023/02/16/18264-agricultores-ya-tienen-financiamiento-con-apoyo-de-fira-en-bolsa-por-mil-800-millones), so FIRA is the most likely funder — **unverified**; no source names it.
- Trajectory: outlook Negative Jul 2023 → Stable Jul 2024 (NPL 4.1%) → Stable Jul 2025 (NPL 5.6%, restructured loans 14.8% of book, top-20 borrowers 2.2x equity) → B/M Jul 2026. The 2025 release already flagged NPL heading to ~MXN 80M by mid-2025 (URLs above).
- Backdrop: Sinaloa agriculture is in a cash crisis — Elizondo himself cancelled Expoceres 2025 in Sep 2024 ("estamos pasando por una crisis en la agricultura", credit "muy limitado"); producers say they cannot repay credits (Sector Primario, c. Jul 2026, https://sectorprimario.mx/2026/07/01/productores-alertan-crisis-financiera-piden-rescate-urgente-para-el-campo/); the federal government is condoning FND debts for 2,298 Sinaloa producers (Luz Noticias, 1 Jun 2026, URL above) — a repayment-culture problem for private lenders; the state government asked 20 parafinancieras on 23 Sep 2026 for a "non-negotiable" 17.5% rate cap — Agrocapital is not among the 18 named attendees (Extraoficial, 23 Sep 2026, https://extraoficial.mx/gobierno-y-parafinancieras-acuerdan-generar-condiciones-para-mejorar-la-agricultura/); the 2026-27 cycle "inicia descapitalizado" (Luz Noticias, 12 Oct 2026, https://www.luznoticias.mx/2026-10-12/agro/ciclo-agricola-2026-2026-inicia-descapitalizado-en-sinaloa/309424).
- Since 2 Nov 2026: nothing new. PCR Verum's issuer page lists no action after 23 Jul 2026 — no withdrawal, no second downgrade, no follow-up report (checked 6 Nov 2026, https://pcrverum.mx/emisor/agrocapital-del-noroeste/). No investor process, adviser or court filing found in Spanish-language search. Deadline: none published; Observación Negativa normally resolves within months, and the 2Q26/3Q26 numbers will decide it.

**What they'll need.** Estimate: uncovered NPL (134.8 − 38.5 = MXN 96.3M) plus foreclosed assets (35.9M) ≈ MXN 132M (~USD 7M) of equity erosion if reserved to 1.0x; to restore a ~17% capital ratio on the current book with full coverage, and to give the funder a reason to un-freeze, **USD 10–15M of equity or deeply subordinated debt** is the working band (USD 10–20M in the record stands, upper end only if the book is to be kept at MXN 1bn). The bigger number is the **MXN 880.6M (~USD 48M) single line**: if the funder wants out, the real transaction is a refinancing/portfolio sale, not a cheque. Alternatives the owner will weigh: (a) Grupo Ceres injects its own capital (group has grain, warehouse and port cash flows — size unverified); (b) orderly run-off: stop originating, collect ~USD 55M of agri loans over 12–24 months, repay the funder, keep the licence; (c) sell the performing book to a larger agri SOFOM or bank. Timing: decision inside 3–6 months, driven by the rating watch and the funder, not by a regulator.

**Why it's ours.** No commercial bank lends to a B-rated, single-funder, unregulated agri SOFOM with 13% NPL in a state where the federal government is forgiving farm debt; bulge-bracket advisers will not run a USD 10–15M process. EGP's readiness-then-mandate model fits the mechanics (restate/reserve the book, build the data room on the loan tape, structure a sub-debt or preferred-equity instrument the funder will accept) and the owner profile (founder-controlled family group with a name and a 65-year franchise to protect). Comparable: the Mexican health-insurer recap — USD 22M from a single investor, financials restated, data room built, inside 60 days after the banks stepped back.

**Regulatory note.** A SOFOM E.N.R. is registered with CNBV/CONDUSEF (AML and consumer-protection regime) but is not supervised for solvency or capital, so there is no regulator forcing a recap and no regulatory deadline — the pressure is the rating watch and the funder covenant. Mexico has no placement-agent licence for a foreign adviser who advises the company on a private placement of its shares or sub-debt to foreign investors outside Mexico; the mandate should be papered as advisory/consulting with success fee, with the offer structured as a private placement to institutional/qualified investors so it stays outside Ley del Mercado de Valores public-offer rules. Local counsel must confirm (Sainz Abogados, Band 1 restructuring; Creel, García-Cuéllar, Aiza y Enríquez; Nader, Hayaux y Goebel — all in db/intermediaries.json). Tax: fees paid by a Mexican resident to a non-resident for independent services are subject to 25% ISR withholding (LISR art. 156) where the service is deemed rendered in Mexico, and any part rendered in Mexico taints the whole unless proven otherwise (IDC Online, 18 May 2026, https://idconline.mx/fiscal-contable/2026/05/18/honorarios-a-extranjeros-cuando-causan-isr-en-mexico-y-sus-excepciones); whether the Mexico–UAE treaty reduces this is **unverified** in this session — price the gross-up into the engagement letter and keep delivery offshore. Desk is not a lawyer.

**Risks.** Be clear about what this is: a B-rated, single-funder, 13%-NPL agri lender in a state where farm credit is being forgiven by the federal government. (1) **Run-off is the base case, not the tail.** With the main line restricted, no new origination, 0.3x reserve cover and cash at MXN 1.5M, the path of least resistance for a 95-year-old founder is to collect the book and repay the development bank — no outside equity, no fee. Probability of run-off or in-house fix: well over half. (2) **Investability of a USD 10–20M equity cheque is doubtful.** A minority equity investor would be buying into an unregulated vehicle, junior to a MXN 880M development-bank line, with a founder family that will not cede control, and with loan-tape quality (restructured loans 14.8% of book in 2025, top-20 borrowers 2–3x equity, likely related-party exposure to Grupo Ceres' own input/machinery customers — unverified, ask) that may be worse than the headline. The investable version is sub-debt or preferred with a funder-side agreement, or a portfolio purchase — a specialist agri-credit fund, not a generalist family office. (3) **Can they pay?** Interest income on a ~MXN 1bn book is roughly MXN 150–200M (~USD 8–11M, estimate); a EUR 25k readiness fee is affordable for the group but will be resisted by a family that has never paid an adviser. (4) **Decision-maker risk:** the only named principal was born in 1931; who actually runs the group and whether the family is aligned is unknown. (5) **Second downgrade / withdrawal** could land any month and would make the book harder to sell. (6) Reputational: Sinaloa; do normal AML/KYC on the group before any engagement letter.
**Bundle/roll-up (Agrocapital + Agropecuaria Financiera + FINDECA):** not realistic as an EGP-led transaction. Three unrelated owners (Grupo Ceres; Agropecuaria Financiera ownership undisclosed, CDMX/Veracruz sugar-cane, BB/M Negative, NPL 18.2%, H1-26 loss MXN 15.7M, single line restricted, https://pcrverum.mx/calificaciones/pcr-verum-baja-a-bb-m-calificacion-de-agropecuaria-financiera-perspectiva-permanece-negativa/ ; FINDECA ownership undisclosed, Oaxaca/Chiapas, BBB-/M Negative but NPL only 0.8% and a MXN 600M line at 98% of funding, https://pcrverum.mx/calificaciones/pcr-verum-baja-a-bbb-m-la-calificacion-de-findecaperspectiva-permanece-negativa/), three crops, three geographies, no common funder named, no sponsor or management platform identified. Merging two distressed single-funder books with one healthy one does not create a fundable BBB platform; it needs a sponsor with a licence and a balance sheet, and EGP has none in db/investors.json. The only usable version of the idea is thematic: one specialist agri-credit investor shown all three as separate opportunities, which is a Hunter note, not a mandate.

**Proposed fee.** Readiness EUR 25k on signature (scope: loan-tape review and reserve restatement, data room, investor memorandum, instrument design — 30 days). Engagement EUR 25k on mandate signature. Success 7% of capital raised (small, hard, competitive-with-nobody). Equity/warrants: 2% of Agrocapital (or of the Grupo Ceres finance holdco) — ask, expect to lose. Potential fee at mid-point: (EUR 25k + EUR 25k) × ~1.08 = ~USD 54k; 7% × USD 15M = USD 1,050k; total ≈ USD 1,104,000 — probability-weighted it is a fraction of that (see Risks).
