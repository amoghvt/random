## Angle
PCR Verum's 23 Jul 2026 cut to B/M with the single development-bank line restricted means Agrocapital either raises USD 10–15M of subordinated capital that the funder will accept or runs the book off; we just restated the financials, built the data room and closed a USD 22M single-investor recapitalisation for a Mexican insurer inside 60 days after its banks stepped back, and we would like 20 minutes to show how it was structured.
Front door: Readiness Programme (EUR 25k, 30 days): loan-tape review and reserve restatement to 1.0x, data room, investor memorandum, instrument design (sub-debt / preferred with a funder-side standstill), plus a "run-off vs recap" comparison the family can show the funder — this last item is what they actually want and is the reason to pay.
Behind the door: Capital Advisory Mandate (EUR 25k + 7% success) + 2% equity/warrants.
Family framing: "capital que conserva el control" — the Elizondo family keeps control; the instrument is designed for the funder, not against the family.

## Fee design
Readiness: EUR 25k on signature. Engagement: EUR 25k on mandate. Success: 7% of capital raised. Equity/warrants: 2% of Agrocapital (or of the finance holdco).
Rationale: small cheque (USD 10–15M), high urgency (rating watch + frozen line), zero competition from banks or bulge-bracket — so the success % sits at the top of the range and the fixed fees at the bottom, because a Sinaloa family that has never paid an adviser will balk at EUR 35k. Arithmetic: (25k + 25k) × ~1.08 ≈ USD 54k + 7% × USD 15M (mid-point of USD 10–20M) = USD 1,050k → potential fee ≈ USD 1,104,000. Price the 25% Mexican withholding on fees for services deemed rendered in Mexico (LISR art. 156) into the engagement letter: deliver offshore, gross-up clause, local counsel to confirm treaty position.

## Kill criteria
- The funder has already called the line or agreed a run-off schedule with the family (then the "deal" is a collection exercise, no capital raise).
- Grupo Ceres intends to inject its own capital and only wants validation.
- Related-party lending to Grupo Ceres customers/affiliates is material and unprovisioned, or the loan tape cannot be produced within two weeks (fraud/opacity indicator → Park, not a client).
- They will not fund the EUR 25k readiness fee this month.
- PCR Verum withdraws the rating or cuts again before the first call with no capital plan in response (signals the family has chosen run-off).
- No one other than the 95-year-old founder can take the decision and he is not reachable.
