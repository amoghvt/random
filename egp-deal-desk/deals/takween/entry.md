## Saudi route — decision memo for Amogh
**(a) Partner with one CMA-licensed Authorised Person as EGP's Saudi execution arm.** On Takween the AP is given: Albilad Capital is financial adviser, lead manager and underwriter (Mubasher, 12 Jul 2026). Under the CMA Securities Business Regulations, "arranging" means introducing parties in relation to an offering of securities "or arrangement of its underwriting, or advises on corporate finance business" (Art. 2(2)); a person is presumed to carry on securities business in the Kingdom if "he engages in the relevant activity with or for a person in the Kingdom" (Art. 4(b)(2)); only an authorised capital market institution may do so (Art. 5; offence under Art. 6), and the CMA's warning page says resulting contracts "are considered to be void". A securities advertisement made to a capital market institution by a person seeking securities business services is excluded (Art. 20(2)) — so the first message goes to Albilad. Economics: a referral/co-advisory share of Albilad's fee on the block EGP fills, paid in Dubai, plus SPV carry from EGP's investors. Cost: Albilad owns the process and the client.
**(b) Co-investment only.** EGP's investors subscribe to sub-underwriting or the rump through Albilad's licensed process; EGP earns only SPV management/carry from its own investors outside the Kingdom. Cleanest, but no issuer-side fee, and it needs appetite for a company with losses at 81.64% of capital.
**Recommendation: (a), with Albilad, structured so (b) is the floor.** Albilad is a repeat rights-issue house (Chemanol, Argaam Dec 2025); one clean deal makes it EGP's standing Saudi arm. **First step:** Amogh calls Albilad's advisory desk (+966 92000 3636, clientservices@albilad-capital.com, public page) asking for the investment banking team on Takween, and proposes a USD 25–40M sub-underwriting commitment from EGP's network against a referral share and an EGP-managed SPV. Saudi counsel must confirm every regulatory statement above before any fee letter.
