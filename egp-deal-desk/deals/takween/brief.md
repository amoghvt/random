**Regulatory note.** CMA Securities Business Regulations (fetched, https://cma.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Regulations/Documents/Amended%20Securities%20Business%20Regulations.pdf): Art. 2(2) "Arranging: a person introduces parties in relation to offering of securities or arrangement of its underwriting, or advises on corporate finance business"; Art. 4(b)(2) a person is presumed to carry on securities business in the Kingdom if "he engages in the relevant activity with or for a person in the Kingdom"; Art. 5 only a CMA-authorised capital market institution or exempt person may do so; Art. 6 contravention is an offence under Art. 60 CML. Art. 9 excludes activity incidental to a non-securities profession only if "not remunerated separately" — a success fee kills that exclusion. CMA warning page (fetched, https://cma.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/PR/Pages/CMA-Warns-Unauthorized-APs.aspx): contracts resulting in securities business by an unauthorised person in the Kingdom "are considered to be void". Art. 20(2)/(4): a securities advertisement made to a capital market institution by a person seeking securities business services, or directed only at CMIs, is excluded — so the first message can go to Albilad. Implication: an EGP success-fee mandate from Takween or Al Othman Holding is securities business in the Kingdom under Art. 4(b)(2) unless local counsel finds otherwise; the fee must be earned through the AP (referral/co-advisory paid by Albilad) or from EGP's own investors (SPV carry) outside the Kingdom. **Saudi counsel required before any fee letter.** The desk is not a lawyer.

**Risks.** (1) Third recapitalisation since 2023 and losses accelerating (Q3-26 SAR 61.7M): the SAR 300M may not be enough — an investor is buying into a turnaround with no CEO in post. (2) The family is already lending (SAR 99M) and may prefer to take up rights and let Albilad carry the rump — then there is no block for us. (3) Albilad may have no interest in sharing economics with an unlicensed Dubai firm. (4) Legal-claims provisions and an unverified USD 14.9M judgment. (5) No CMA filing after 4.5 months — could stall into 2027 or be re-cut again. (6) CMA's 31 Oct 2026 draft (consultation to 28 Nov) would force companies with losses ≥50% to publish recovery plans and top-20 holders (Arab News, https://www.arabnews.com/finance/saudi-regulator-proposes-giving-shareholders-more-say-over-loss-making-firms-and-boards-3008840) — more scrutiny, but also more transparency for us. (7) Fee-payer capacity: listed company is loss-making; the holdco can pay, the listed entity's payments to an unlicensed adviser are the void-contract risk.
