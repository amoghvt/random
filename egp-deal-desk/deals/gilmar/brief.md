# Gilmar Divisione Industria S.p.A. (Iceberg / Paolo Pecora licensee; N°21 exited) — Italy, fashion              Score: 62  [Watch]
As of: 2026-11-06

**What they are.** Gilmar Divisione Industria S.p.A., Via Malpasso 723/725, San Giovanni in Marignano (Rimini): the Gerani family's manufacturing/licensing company (founded 1992), which designs, produces and distributes Iceberg and Paolo Pecora and did contract manufacturing for luxury maisons; 256 direct employees at 31 Jul 2026 plus c. 160 subcontract workshops (Pambianco, 23 Oct 2026, https://www.pambianconews.com/2026/10/23/gilmar-avvia-la-composizione-negoziata-della-crisi-debiti-per-372-milioni-498415/). 2024 revenue EUR 42.16M (2023: EUR 46.07M), EBITDA c. –EUR 3M, net loss EUR 5.03M, book equity c. EUR 40.75M; 2019 production value EUR 95.9M (BeBeez, 4 Nov 2026, https://bebeez.it/crisi-rilanci/gilmar-famiglia-gerani-avvia-la-composizione-negoziata-della-crisi-il-24-novembre-ludienza-sulle-misure-protettive-in-vendita-fabbrica-e-ramo-produttivo/; reportaziende.it, https://www.reportaziende.it/gilmar_divisione_industria_spa_rn). 100% owned by Gerani Group S.p.A. (Gerani family). IMPORTANT correction to the sweep: the Iceberg trademark sits in a separate company, Gilmar S.p.A., also controlled by Gerani Group and NOT in the procedure (BeBeez, 4 Nov 2026, URL above). Decision-makers: Silvano Gerani (Presidente) and Paolo Gerani (Amministratore Delegato, Direttore Artistico) (gilmar.it, https://www.gilmar.it/; Chiamami Città, 22 Oct 2026, https://www.chiamamicitta.it/gilmar-profondo-rosso-256-dipendenti-a-rischio-e-congelamento-creditori-lazienda-ha-debiti-per-37-milioni-di-euro/).

**What's wrong.**
- 29 Sep 2026: request for appointment of the esperto filed on the national platform; esperto Dott. Mauro Morelli accepted 6 Oct 2026; 7 Oct 2026 petition under art. 19 CCII to the Tribunale di Rimini (RG 1734/2026) for protective measures of up to 120 days against all creditors except employees; judge Dott. Filippo Meneghello, decree 12 Oct 2026; hearing 24 Nov 2026, 09:00 (Chiamami Città, 22 Oct 2026, URL above).
- Total debt EUR 37.2M at 31 Jul 2026 (EUR 36.63M at 31 Aug), > 800 creditors; breakdown: related Gerani companies EUR 11.88M (subordinated to third parties), privileged EUR 8.59M, trade suppliers EUR 8.11M, banks EUR 5.86M (of which EUR 5.29M MCC/SACE-guaranteed term loans), advances EUR 2.07M (Chiamami Città, 22 Oct 2026; Milano Finanza, 23 Oct 2026, https://www.milanofinanza.it/fashion/gilmar-al-via-la-composizione-negoziata-debiti-per-37-2-milioni-e-piano-di-risanamento-202610231457342725; MF print 27 Oct 2026, https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/gilmar-debiti-per-37-2-milioni-e-risanamento-2722099).
- Causes: Missoni and Siviglia licences terminated by the licensors (c. EUR 25M revenue/year lost); volumes roughly halved since 2020; 35,000 m² plant and 256 staff sized for twice the turnover (FashionUnited, 22 Oct 2026, https://fashionunited.it/news/business/gilmar-ha-debiti-per-37-2-milioni-di-euro-la-contrazione-dei-ricavi-dai-marchi-in-licenza-tra-le-ragioni-della-crisi/2026102232001).
- Workforce: all 256 on cassa integrazione since July 2026; Regione Emilia-Romagna crisis table (6 Jul 2026) moving CIGO to CIGS (Il Resto del Carlino, 27 Oct 2026, https://www.ilrestodelcarlino.it/rimini/cronaca/la-gilmar-in-crisi-si-bda4aca7; Regione E-R, 6 Jul 2026, https://notizie.regione.emilia-romagna.it/comunicati/2026/lavoro-vertenza-gruppo-gilmar-di-san-giovanni-in-marignano-rn-verso-la-prosecuzione-degli-ammortizzatori-sociali-per-la-storica-impresa-riminese-del-fashion-lassessore-paglia-prendere-ulteriore-tempo-e-la-condizione-necessaria-per-consentire-allazienda-di).
- 2 Nov 2026: Gilmar's 30% of 2112 srl (N°21) sold to Style Capital (Roberta Benaglia); price undisclosed (Il Sole 24 Ore, 2 Nov 2026, https://ilsole24ore.com/art/n21-lascia-gilmar-ed-entra-style-capital-il-distretto-rimini-si-aggrava-crisi-AJXgvw1B; Il Resto del Carlino, 4 Nov 2026, https://www.ilrestodelcarlino.it/rimini/cronaca/aeffe-e-gilmar-la-moda-0aab2554).
- Deadline: 24 Nov 2026 hearing; protection, if confirmed, runs 120 days (to roughly Feb–Mar 2027). Creditors may file briefs up to 5 days before.

**What they'll need.** The filed plan (advisor Studio Aicardi & Partners) is a controlled wind-down, not a recap: (1) transfer of the "ramo conto terzi" (contract manufacturing, 126 staff, up to 185 with logistics) to an industrial buyer with court authorisation under art. 22 CCII; (2) exit from the licence business after AW-2026 orders; (3) sale of the unencumbered 35,000 m² plant to pay third-party creditors. Realisable asset value estimated by the company at EUR 25–33M vs EUR 37.2M debt; inventory EUR 15M at 31 Aug after a 29.6% write-down (Chiamami Città, 22 Oct 2026). BeBeez (4 Nov 2026) reports contacts with industrial investors started, no deal. Estimate: the realistic "capital" is EUR 10–20M of disposal proceeds (branch + real estate), plus — outside the procedure — a possible partner/buyer for the Iceberg brand at Gilmar S.p.A. (estimate; not announced by the family). Timing: buyer for the branch needed inside the 120-day window.

**Why it's ours.** Banks are already minor creditors (EUR 5.9M, state-guaranteed) and will not lead; the bulge bracket will not touch a EUR 42M-revenue contract manufacturer in a Rimini district with Aeffe and Fuzzi failing simultaneously (Il Resto del Carlino, 23 Oct 2026, https://www.ilrestodelcarlino.it/rimini/cronaca/la-moda-e-in-ginocchio-f93a32f6). EGP's fit is partial: a data room and a structured buyer process for the branch/plant, and an investor introduction for Iceberg (family office / brand-platform type), are what we do; but the readiness-then-recap model assumes a going concern to recapitalise, which here is only the Iceberg company, not the entity in crisis. Comparable: Mexican health-insurer recap — USD 22M from a single investor inside 60 days after the banks stepped back.

**Who decides.** Entrepreneur keeps control in a composizione negoziata (art. 21 CCII); Silvano Gerani (Presidente) and Paolo Gerani (Amministratore Delegato, operational lead) sign; Giuliana Marchini and Patrizia Gerani sit on the board (gilmar.it, https://www.gilmar.it/). Financial/industrial advisor to the plan: Studio Aicardi & Partners — Dott. Piero Aicardi (founder) and Achille Mazzanti (Senior Partner), offices Bologna, Milan and Rimini (Chiamami Città, 22 Oct 2026; firm site, https://www.aicardiepartners.it/; iusletter, 20 Mar 2026, https://iusletter.com/news/la-scala-e-aicardi-partner-advisor-nella-composizione-negoziata-della-crisi-da-12-mln-nella-gdo-leggi-la-rassegna-stampa-dedicata/). Esperto indipendente: Dott. Mauro Morelli — a search-result CV places a Mauro Morelli, dottore commercialista since 1971, Senior Partner at Delphi, Bologna (https://fondazionedelmonte.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/CV-Morelli-Mauro-1-1.pdf — page returned 403; identity match unverified). Legal counsel to the company: not named in any retrieved source. Public contact paths: company switchboard and contact form at https://www.gilmar.it/contacts (HQ +39 0541 959111, info@gilmar.it as published there); Aicardi & Partners contact page https://aicardiepartners.it/contatti/ (general mailbox posta@aicardiepartners.it as published; Rimini office Via Soardi 5).

**Regulatory note.** Italy is MiFID II territory (TUF, Consob): placing or arranging financial instruments is a reserved activity. Structure as a corporate-finance advisory agreement with the company (readiness + success fee on capital or proceeds), with EGP's investors introduced from Dubai and contracting directly; no marketing of securities in Italy. In a composizione negoziata the entrepreneur keeps ordinary and extraordinary management, but must inform the esperto of extraordinary acts and needs Tribunale authorisation (art. 22 CCII) for the branch transfer and for any priority new finance; the esperto facilitates, does not run a sale. So the first message goes to the company's advisor (Aicardi & Partners) or to Paolo Gerani — not to the esperto, who is neutral and must not be lobbied. Local counsel needed: yes (Rimini/Bologna restructuring counsel) for the advisory agreement, for fee priority inside the procedure, and for any investor term sheet.

**Risks.** Blunt: (1) This is a wind-down with an asset sale, not a recapitalisation — the family's own plan is to stop licensed production, sell the factory and sell the contract-manufacturing branch; an "equity partner" for the entity in crisis is not what they are asking for. (2) The Iceberg brand is in Gilmar S.p.A., outside the procedure; the family has not put it on the market; a brand partner is a Gerani-family M&A decision, and a fashion-brand sale in a district contagion (Aeffe, Fuzzi) will be priced as distressed. (3) Ability to pay: the company says it cannot pay its 800 creditors and the whole workforce is on CIGS; a readiness fee would have to come from Gerani Group (itself a EUR 11.9M subordinated creditor) — feasible but a hard ask, and inside the procedure the esperto will question any new advisory fee. (4) Window: 24 Nov hearing, then 120 days; advisors (Aicardi & Partners) already hold the plan; any buyer contacts are theirs — EGP arrives late and can only add buyers/investors, not the process. (5) Related-party debt and intra-group licences (Gilmar S.p.A. licenses Iceberg to the crisis company) will be examined by creditors; expect disputes over the brand's value staying with the family while trade creditors take a haircut. (6) Liquidation risk if the branch buyer fails or the court refuses protection on 24 Nov. No fraud indicators seen in retrieved sources.

**Proposed fee.** Readiness EUR 25k on signature (data room + buyer/investor memorandum for the branch, plant and Iceberg) / Engagement EUR 25k / Success 6% of capital raised or proceeds introduced by EGP (accept 4–5% on real-estate proceeds) / Equity: 1–2% warrants in Gilmar S.p.A. (Iceberg) if a brand partner is brought in. Arithmetic (EUR/USD 1.08 assumed): (EUR 25k + EUR 25k) × 1.08 = USD 54k; 6% × EUR 15M mid-point of EUR 10–20M = EUR 0.9M = USD 972k; potential_fee_usd ≈ USD 1,026,000 — probability-weighted it is far lower (see Risks).
