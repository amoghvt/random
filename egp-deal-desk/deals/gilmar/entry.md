## Fee design
Readiness: EUR 25k on signature.  Engagement: EUR 25k.  Success: 6% of capital raised (accept 4–5% on real-estate proceeds).  Equity/warrants: 1–2% of Gilmar S.p.A. (Iceberg holdco).
Rationale: small ticket (EUR 10–20M), company cannot pay its creditors so the fee must come from Gerani Group — keep the readiness fee at the bottom of the range; competition is low (no banker, the advisor is a commercialista) but the time window is short and the outcome is a disposal, so the upside is in a success fee on introduced proceeds and in the Iceberg warrants, not in the fixed fee. Potential fee: USD 54k fixed + USD 972k success (6% × EUR 15M × 1.08) ≈ USD 1.03M; probability-weighted well under USD 300k.
