# Cruz Suiza Compañía de Seguros S.A. — Argentina, personal-lines insurer (life / AP / health / sepelio) with new nationwide P&C licence              Score: 43  [Park]
As of: 2026-11-06

**What they are.** Buenos Aires insurer (SSN registration 0553, "más de 25 años" in the market; lines on its own site: vida, accidentes personales, salud, sepelio, saldo deudor — i.e. personal lines, not a P&C book as the 2 Nov sweep labelled it) that in 2026 obtained SSN authorisation (RESOL-2026-53) to write "Otros Riesgos de Daños Patrimoniales" nationwide (flood, quake, liability, machinery, cyber) (cruzsuiza.com, retrieved 6 Nov 2026, https://www.cruzsuiza.com/ ; iProfesional, 27 May 2026, https://www.iprofesional.com/negocios/455816-seguros-en-crisis-empresa-marcelo-figueiras-jose-urtubey-fue-sancionada-por-el-gobierno). Size (premiums, capital, staff) is not disclosed in any retrieved source — unverified. Bought March 2024 by Marcelo Figueiras (president of Cruz Suiza; majority shareholder of Laboratorios Richmond with 38.54% per Richmond's own investors page, https://www.richmondlab.com/inversores/) and José Urtubey (vice-president; Celulosa Argentina), who with Alberto Serventich also own the surety insurer ACG (iProfesional, 27 May 2026, URL above). Cruz Suiza's registered footer address, Bouchard 680 2º, is Richmond's headquarters address (cruzsuiza.com; https://www.richmondlab.com/contacto/).

**What's wrong.** Less than the sweep thought — the regulatory problem has been cured.
- 26 May 2026 — SSN RESOL-2026-230-APN-SSN#MEC: minimum-capital deficit of ARS 48,633,437 in the 31 Mar 2026 statements; art. 31 requerimiento; Plan de Regularización y Saneamiento due in 15 calendar days; art. 86 inhibición general de bienes (bank current accounts excluded). The company had asked on 13 May for an extension to the 30 Jun 2026 balance citing "iliquidez transitoria" from growth and claims payments; the Gerencia de Evaluación refused because future capital contributions are not "información patrimonial cierta" (resolution PDF retrieved and read in full: https://100seguro.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/230-SUN-BO.pdf ; 100% Seguro, 27 May 2026, https://100seguro.com.ar/la-ssn-aplica-medidas-cautelares-sobre-aseguradora-y-exige-plan-para-recomponer-su-capital/).
- 27–28 May 2026 — shareholders paid in irrevocable contributions of exactly ARS 48,633,437, pro rata to their stakes, and notified the SSN on 28 May (RE-2026-53027490) (iProfesional, 29 May 2026, https://www.iprofesional.com/negocios/455923-seguros-crisis-marcelo-figueiras-recolecto-mas-de-48-millones-pesos-para-salvar-cruz-suiza ; Res. 251 considerandos, below).
- 8 Jun 2026 (published 11 Jun) — SSN RESOL-2026-251-APN-SSN#MEC: "levantamiento definitivo" of the inhibition; Gerencia de Evaluación found the contributions sufficient, Gerencia de Inspección verified they were actually paid in, the SSN states the insurer is back in "condiciones normales de funcionamiento" (art. 86). No residual conditions, no plan approval step mentioned (resolution PDF retrieved and read in full: https://elseguroenaccion.com.ar/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/251-SUN-BO.pdf ; El Seguro en Acción, 11 Jun 2026, https://elseguroenaccion.com.ar/cruz-suiza-normalizo-su-situacion-patrimonial-y-la-ssn-levanto-la-inhibicion-de-bienes/ ; 100% Seguro, 12 Jun 2026, https://100seguro.com.ar/la-ssn-levanto-la-inhibicion-general-de-bienes-que-pesaba-sobre-aseguradora/).
- Scale of the hole: ARS 48.6M at the BNA selling rate of ARS 1,405/USD at end-April 2026 (El Cronista, 2 May 2026, https://www.cronista.com/finanzas-mercados/el-dolar-cerro-abril-sin-cambios-respecto-de-marzo-que-espera-el-mercado-para-mayo-y-el-resto-del-ano/) is roughly USD 35,000 (desk arithmetic). The sector-wide new floor is 750,000 UVA ≈ USD 857k, fully applicable from June 2026 (Abeledo Gottheil / GILC, 15 Jan 2026, https://www.globalinsurancelaw.com/2026/01/15/regulator-moves-to-prevent-insolvencies-in-argentinas-insurance-market/). Both are an order of magnitude below EGP's band.

The pattern is right (regulated, owner-run, regulator-forced capital call, no bank will lead for a company that was under art. 86 in May; Argentine FX risk keeps bulge-bracket out) and the owners have already shown they answer the SSN with their own cheque within 48 hours — which is exactly why they do not need us. Plan Seguro was USD 22M with a live regulatory wound; this wound was USD 35k and is closed. EGP fits only as (a) a growth-equity partner for the Cruz Suiza + ACG platform if the owners prefer a partner to self-funding, or (b) a UAE-foothold / regional-investor relationship with Figueiras for the next, larger Richmond-group need (Richmond itself: listed, ON issuer — not assessed here).

**Regulatory note.** Argentina. (1) Any capital contribution into an insurer is reported to and verified by the SSN (Res. 251 shows the Gerencia de Inspección checking the aportes); share transfers/changes of control in an insurer need prior SSN authorisation under Ley 20.091 and the RGAA — desk understanding, not verified against the text this session; local counsel to confirm. (2) Advising a private company on raising capital is not in itself a licensed activity in Argentina (CNV regulates public offerings and registered agents, not private M&A/capital advisory) — desk understanding, unverified; Abeledo Gottheil (Luis Matías Ponferrada, senior partner, heads insurance; +54 11 4516-1500 ext. 277 per https://www.globalinsurancelaw.com/members/abeledo-gottheil/ ; firm contact page https://abeledogottheil.com.ar/contacto/ , estudio@abeledogottheil.com.ar) is the right confirming counsel and is already in db/intermediaries.json. (3) Transfer risk is real: a foreign investor's USD comes in through the MLC and its exit is governed by BCRA Exterior y Cambios rules; payments of services to non-resident related parties wait 90 days and several MLC windows still need prior BCRA approval (Com. "A" 8481, 14 Sep 2026, per Allende & Brea, https://allende.com/bancario/el-bcra-flexibiliza-el-cobro-de-exportaciones-de-servicios-y-ajusta-reglas-de-acceso-al-mercado-de-cambios-09-15-2026/). EGP's fee invoiced from Dubai to Cruz Suiza S.A. would be an onshore service import needing MLC access with contract and invoice; whether the owners hold a non-Argentine entity that could pay offshore is unverified. Structure the fee so that it is payable in USD from outside Argentina or not at all; do not accept ARS onshore. (4) No UAE issue: pure advisory from DMCC/mainland, no DFSA/FSRA arranging activity, provided no UAE-regulated investor is solicited through a regulated channel.

**Risks.** Blunt: (a) The signal is dead — cured in 48 hours, lifted "definitivamente" in 13 days; there is no regulator pressing. (b) The owners are wealthy industrialists who just proved they self-fund; a USD 1–5M growth cheque is pocket change against Richmond's balance sheet, so the probability they pay a readiness fee to an unknown Dubai adviser is low. (c) Below band: the regulatory floor is < USD 1M and even a generous expansion budget is at the bottom of the band; 7% of a USD 3M raise is USD 210k. (d) Getting paid: BCRA transfer constraints on fees and on an investor's exit; an investor may demand a holdco outside Argentina, which the owners may not want. (e) Sub-scale, personal-lines book moving into P&C under a UVA-indexed floor: the "transitory illiquidity" story of May could recur at any quarterly balance — that is the only reason to keep watching. (f) Opacity: no premiums, no audited numbers retrieved; the SSN's own words in Res. 230 note "ulteriores observaciones que pudieran surgir". (g) Group risk: Richmond's own leverage (ON issuer) is not assessed here — unverified. No fraud indicators found.

**Proposed fee.** Readiness EUR 25k on signature (floor of the range; payable in USD/EUR offshore) / Engagement EUR 0 (waived, rolled into success) / Success 7% of capital raised with a USD 150k minimum / Equity 2% warrants in the insurance holdco. Potential fee: EUR 25k ≈ USD 29k (at ~1.16, assumed) + 7% × USD 3M mid-point (of USD 1–5M growth estimate) = USD 210k → USD 239k. Probability-weighted it is closer to zero than to that.
