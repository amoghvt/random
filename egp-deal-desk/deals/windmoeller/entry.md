## Kill criteria
- The plan is a creditor haircut funded by the family and real estate, with no outside capital wanted.
- An M&A/investor adviser has already been mandated (and the Sachwalter backs them).
- The Sachwalter refuses any advisory fee as an estate liability and the family cannot pay it outside the estate.
- Banks have withdrawn and the case is heading to an asset deal (Regelinsolvenz) rather than a plan.
- Off-book liabilities or an unexplained reason for two CEO exits in 2026 that the family will not discuss.
- Trade-channel revenue has fallen so far that FY2026 EBITDA is negative with no credible 2027 bridge — the ticket becomes a rescue, not a recap.
