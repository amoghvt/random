# Windmöller GmbH (wineo) — Germany, flooring / acoustic solutions              Score: 71  [Priority]
As of: 2026-11-06

**What they are.** Detmold (NRW) maker of floor coverings and acoustic underlays under the umbrella brand wineo (PURLINE bio-flooring); third-generation family business, >75 years, FY2025 revenue c. EUR 83M, c. 280 staff, subsidiaries in Poland and the USA plus a worldwide sales network (GÖRG release, 15 Oct 2026, https://www.goerg.de/de/aktuelles/veroeffentlichungen/15-10-2026/windmoeller-gmbh-saniert-sich-ueber-ein-schutzschirmverfahren); FY2025 EBITDA EUR 3.7M (NKP M&A Insights, 16 Oct 2026, https://www.mainsights.io/ma-news/german-flooring-manufacturer-windmoller-enters-restructuring-under-insolvency-protection). Sole shareholder of record is Windmöller Holding GmbH (Northdata, HRB 6249 AG Lemgo, https://www.northdata.com/Windm%C3%B6ller%20GmbH,%20Detmold/Amtsgericht%20Lemgo%20HRB%206249), whose shareholders are Matthias and Annika Windmöller (Parkett Magazin, 11 Jun 2024, https://www.parkettmagazin.de/willkommen-seriennummer-110266.htm). Decision-maker: Matthias Windmöller, CEO and managing partner, back in the chair since early October 2026 (Holz-Forum Magazin, 12 Oct 2026, https://holzforummagazin.de/willkommen-seriennummer-119205-lang-de.htm; company site, https://www.windmoeller.de/unternehmen/willkommen).

**What's wrong.**
- 9 Oct 2026: Amtsgericht Detmold, case 50 IN 209/26, ordered Schutzschirm protective measures (§§ 270b, 270d InsO), appointed Dr Yorck T. Streitbörger (Bielefeld) preliminary Sachwalter, approved in principle a management service contract dated 8 Oct 2026 for the "Insolvenz-Geschäftsführer", set a plan deadline of three months from service of the order (i.e. around mid-January 2027) and asked for the Sachwalter's first report within two weeks (Übernahme-Radar register notice, updated 18 Oct 2026, https://uebernahmeradar.de/unternehmen/windmoeller-gmbh-hrb-6249-detmold?lang=en; Insolvenz-Radar, https://insolvenz-radar.de/firmeninsolvenz/HRB+6249/Detmold/R2303/). No opening of main proceedings, no creditors' committee, no plan filing reported as of today.
- Trigger: a sharp fall in demand in the flooring trade channel over the three months to October 2026, blamed on the German construction crisis; new channels had not yet replaced the lost trade business; the company was hit "after more than two years of restructuring" (GÖRG release, 15 Oct 2026; NW, 13 Oct 2026, https://www.nw.de/lokal/kreis_lippe/uebersicht/24439550_Detmolder-Unternehmen-Windmoeller-verzeichnet-drastische-Einbussen-und-zieht-die-Reissleine.html). The §270d certificate by Alexander Demant (dhs, Detmold) confirms no Zahlungsunfähigkeit and that the restructuring is not obviously hopeless (GÖRG release).
- Prior restructuring 2023–2026: Augustdorf plant closed and consolidated into Detmold, c. 100 jobs cut to c. 250, social plan agreed, Bad Oeynhausen site spun off to a family member, a plant with office building already sold to an investor (NW, 7 Oct 2024, https://www.nw.de/nachrichten/wirtschaft/23957355_Unternehmen-Windmoeller-verlaesst-Augustdorf-Stellen-fallen-weg.html). Restructuring CEO Jürgen Abromeit (ex-CEO Indus Holding AG) took 30% in May 2024 (EUWID, 13 Jun 2024, https://www.euwid-holz.de/news/unternehmen/neuer-ceo-abromeit-beteiligt-sich-mit-30-an-windmoeller-130624/), declared the company "back in the black" and handed over to Philip Lehmann as CEO/CRO in July 2026, returning his shares to the family "as agreed" (boden wand decke, 15 Jul 2026, https://www.boden-wand-decke.de/fuehrungswechsel-bei-windmoeller-356117/; SN-Home, 14 Jul 2026, https://www.sn-home.de/willkommen-seriennummer-17110.htm). Lehmann was appointed 31 Jul 2026 and was gone by the 30 Sep 2026 register publication, which already lists Frederik Wittop as managing director (Northdata). Three CEOs in three months before the filing.
- Deadline: insolvency plan due c. mid-January 2027 (three months from service of the 9 Oct order). The CEO's stated target is to be "future-proof again in the course of next year" (GÖRG release).

**What they'll need.** Estimate: EUR 10–25M (≈ USD 11–27M at an assumed 1.08, unverified) of plan financing — fresh equity or a holdco/plan loan to fund the plan quota, working capital for the trade-channel rebuild and the already announced 2026–27 investment programme in processes, products and sales (raumausstatter.com, 16 Jul 2026, https://raumausstatter.com/windmoeller-stellt-sich-neu-auf/). Instrument most likely to fit the family: minority recapitalisation (25–49%) at Windmöller GmbH or Holding level, possibly with a sale-and-leaseback of the Detmold site (Charles-Lindbergh-Ring 13). Timing: investor must be visible before the plan is filed (January 2027) and funded on plan confirmation (Q1–Q2 2027). No bank debt figures, no creditor list and no new-money quantum have been published — all of this is estimate.

**Why it's ours.** Second restructuring inside three years, construction-cycle exposure, EBITDA margin of c. 4.5% and a CEO carousel: house banks will not lead new money and bulge-bracket advisers will not touch an EUR 83M flooring company for a EUR 10–25M ticket. The advisers on the file (GÖRG, Montag & Montag, Reitze Recker Hagemann, Streitbörger) are process people, not an investor network — no M&A adviser or investor process has been announced in any source retrieved (searched 6 Nov 2026 in German and English). EGP's readiness-then-mandate model fits: 30 days to restate FY2026 numbers around the trade-channel loss, build the data room and write the memorandum the Sachwalter and creditors will need anyway; then a mandate to bring one family-office/private-group investor. Comparable: Plan Seguro — USD 22M from a single investor, financials restated and data room built, inside 60 days after the banks stepped back.

**Regulatory note.** Germany, Schutzschirm (§270d InsO), preliminary stage. Management (Windmöller + Wittop as Insolvenz-Geschäftsführer) stays in control and runs any investor process; the preliminary Sachwalter supervises and must consent to material transactions and estate liabilities; the court set the plan deadline and will later form a creditors' committee. The first message therefore goes to the CRO (Wittop), with the Sachwalter informed, not bypassed. Any investor comes in through the Insolvenzplan (§225a InsO allows shareholder-rights changes and debt-equity measures) or a pre-plan capital increase approved by the Sachwalter. EGP's advisory-with-success-fee mandate: GmbH shares and plan loans are not MiFID II financial instruments, so corporate-finance advice on this structure is generally outside the BaFin licence perimeter; if the instrument becomes a bond, Schuldschein or convertible, or if EGP markets securities to EU investors, the MiFID/WpIG perimeter is engaged and EGP (Dubai, no EU passport) would need a licensed partner. German counsel is needed for: (i) fee-letter enforceability inside the proceeding (fee must be approved as Masseverbindlichkeit by the Sachwalter), (ii) equity/warrant ask at Holding level, (iii) the licence question. The desk is not a lawyer.

**Risks.**
- The process people may already own the investor track: Montag & Montag advertises "arranging alternative financing sources on short notice" and distressed M&A (https://www.montag-montag.de/); Taras's own recent files include an investor solution for PICARD (profile). EGP could be seen as a competitor rather than a source of money. Position as investor-side partner with a referral fee, not as a process adviser.
- Fee affordability: the §270d certificate says liquidity is sufficient, but any readiness fee is an estate liability needing Sachwalter consent; expect pushback on EUR 30k up front and a request to defer into the plan.
- Plan may be a pure creditor haircut funded by the family and a sale-and-leaseback, with no outside equity — then there is no mandate.
- Governance churn: Abromeit (30%, out), Lehmann (two months), family back. An outside investor will ask why two professional CEOs left in 2026; the answer is not in any public source.
- Construction cycle: the trade channel that collapsed in Q3 2026 may not come back; a 2027 plan built on recovery will struggle with investors.
- No creditor, bank or debt figures are public; the need band is an estimate until the first call.
- Succession is a possible angle but unverified: no source says whether the family wants to keep control long term; the Abromeit "shares returned as agreed" line suggests the family wanted control back, which supports a minority-recap pitch.
- Re-verification moved the score from 76 to 71: Bank-shyness is weaker than first scored (a cyclical distress, no regulatory/tax/claims complication), and Size fit is capped by the Sachwalter-consent question on fees. Still Priority.
