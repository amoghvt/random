## Fee design
Readiness: EUR 25k on signature.  Engagement: EUR 25k.  Success: 5% of capital raised from EGP-introduced investors.  Equity/warrants: 1% of the new holdco.
Rationale: (1) The estate pays and every adviser fee needs Sachwalter/committee consent — anchor low on fixed fees and take upside on success; a 7% ask next to a mandated German M&A adviser will be refused. (2) Urgency is extreme (weeks) and competition for the sell-side seat is real, so EGP's defensible slot is investor-side with territory-limited exclusivity; accept a success-only variant if the committee refuses any fixed fee, but then at 6%.
Arithmetic: fixed EUR 50k ≈ USD 58k (assumed EUR/USD 1.16); success 5% × midpoint of EUR 15–40M = EUR 27.5M ≈ USD 31.9M → USD 1.595M; potential fee ≈ USD 1.65M.
