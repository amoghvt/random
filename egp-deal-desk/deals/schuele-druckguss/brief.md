# Julius Schüle Druckguss GmbH — Germany, aluminium die-casting (auto Tier-2)              Score: 70  [Priority — borderline; narrow window]
As of: 2026-11-06

**What they are.** Family-run (since 1951) aluminium die-caster in Schwäbisch Gmünd (Buchstraße 195, 73525), FY2025 group revenue c. EUR 110.9M, c. 680 staff of which 152 in Germany; two producing subsidiaries, Schüle Slovakia s.r.o. (Poprad, since 1999) and Schüle Polska Sp. z o.o. (Świebodzice, since 2016), act as "verlängerte Werkbank" for the German company (INDat, 21 Sep 2026, https://www.der-indat.de/julius-schuele-druckguss-gmbh-stellt-antrag-auf-anordnung-eines-schutzschirmverfahrens/; Gmünder Tagespost, 22 Sep 2026, https://www.gmuender-tagespost.de/ostalb/schwaebisch-gmuend/stadt-schwaebisch-gmuend/schuele-druckguss-insolvenz-eigenverwaltung-in-schwaebisch-gmuend-94504409.html). Automotive is roughly three-quarters of sales; customers named in trade press include ZF, Bosch, Hella, Brose, Magna, Festo; business units Mobility/Industry/Facility/Medicine/Lifestyle (Foundry-Planet, 2 Jun 2025, https://www.foundry-planet.com/d/foundry-of-excellence-schuele-group-expands-its-portfolio-die-casting-expertise-for-new-markets/). Operating MD is Michael Eidecker; the holding is Julius Schüle GmbH (not in the proceedings) and the owning family members are not named in any retrieved source (unverified). Legal control of the process sits with Generalbevollmächtigter Martin Mucha (GRUB BRUGGER) and vorläufiger Sachwalter Arndt Geiwitz (SGP Schneider Geiwitz).

**What's wrong.**
- 18 Sep 2026: filed for Eigenverwaltung at AG Aalen; Schutzschirmverfahren (§ 270d InsO) ordered the same day for the German GmbH only; PL/SK subsidiaries have not filed and are "nicht betroffen" (INDat, 21 Sep 2026, URL above; SGP news, 22 Sep 2026, https://www.schneidergeiwitz.de/de/news/articles/julius-schule-druckguss-gmbh-arndt-geiwitz-zum-vorlaufigen-sachwalter-bestellt).
- Losses: EUR 4.8M (2023), EUR 9.6M (2024), per North Data as cited by the local press (Gmünder Tagespost, 22 Sep 2026; drweb.de, 24 Sep 2026, https://www.drweb.de/schuele-druckguss-schutzschirm-schwaebisch-gmuend/). Causes stated by management: low-wage competition, overcapacity, falling automotive volumes and margins (INDat). 42 jobs were already at risk in 2024 on weak orders (Gmünder Tagespost, 22 Sep 2026).
- Insolvenzgeld covers German wages only through November 2026; Mucha: no guarantee beyond that (Gmünder Tagespost, 5 Oct 2026, https://www.gmuender-tagespost.de/ostalb/schwaebisch-gmuend/stadt-schwaebisch-gmuend/schuele-druckguss-insolvenz-gmuend-zukunft-und-investorensuche-94521832.html).
- Deadlines: plan must be presented within at most three months of the order (§ 270d InsO), i.e. by about 18 Dec 2026 (drweb.de, 24 Sep 2026); Mucha expects the main proceedings to open on 1 Dec 2026 (Gmünder Tagespost, 5 Oct 2026).
- Investor process: as of 5 Oct 2026 a "strukturierter M&A-Prozess" is to start "kurzfristig"; first an M&A adviser must be selected in coordination with the preliminary creditors' committee and the Sachwalter; no adviser, no bidder, no plan filing announced (Gmünder Tagespost, 5 Oct 2026). No newer public report found through 6 Nov 2026 (searches 6 Nov 2026; see Sources).

**What they'll need.** Estimate: EUR 15–40M of new money — plan financing for the German OpCo plus an equity sponsor who takes over the holding/group (DE site + PL/SK plants, which are explicitly meant to stay outside insolvency because a filing there would hit customer supply, Gmünder Tagespost, 5 Oct 2026). Instrument: majority/minority equity via Insolvenzplan (debt-equity swap or plan sponsor) or, failing that, an asset deal (übertragende Sanierung) of the German business. Timing: investor must be visible before/around opening on 1 Dec 2026 and plan filing by mid/late Dec 2026; closing Q1 2027. All amounts are estimates — no balance sheet, debt figure or liquidity figure is public.

**Why it's ours.** Banks won't lead: company is loss-making two years running, inside a Schutzschirm, in an auto-casting market with overcapacity (press cites the AE Group precedent of a long, hard investor search, Gmünder Tagespost, 5 Oct 2026). Bulge-bracket won't touch a EUR 111M die-caster. EGP's fit is specific: the appointed M&A adviser will canvass the usual German strategics and PE distressed funds; EGP brings a different buyer type — EU/GCC family offices and industrial groups that want a European aluminium casting platform with a low-cost PL/SK footprint — and the readiness work (English IM, restated group numbers, data room supplement) that a non-EU investor needs. Comparable: Plan Seguro — restated financials, data room, USD 22M single-investor recap inside 60 days after the banks stepped back.

**Regulatory note.** Germany. (1) Who controls: in Eigenverwaltung the debtor's management (Eidecker, with Mucha as Generalbevollmächtigter) runs the business and the investor process; the Sachwalter (Geiwitz) supervises for the creditors and, with the preliminary creditors' committee, must sign off on material steps including the choice of the M&A adviser (as Mucha himself describes it, Gmünder Tagespost, 5 Oct 2026). Any EGP fee owed by the German GmbH would be an estate liability needing Mucha's engagement and the Sachwalter's/committee's consent; the owning family cannot commit the estate alone. Therefore the first message goes to Mucha, with Geiwitz as second route; the family/holding is a third route for a holdco-level deal only. (2) Licensing: arranging or placing securities/shares for a German issuer is a MiFID II investment service (Anlagevermittlung / Platzierungsgeschäft under WpIG/KWG, BaFin-licensed). EGP positions as advisory-with-success-fee (readiness work, structuring, investor introductions originating from outside the EU, no client money, no holding-out as placement agent in Germany, investor-side NDAs and reverse-enquiry documentation). Any German-law success-fee or finder agreement should be reviewed by German counsel before signature; if an EU-licensed intermediary is needed, use the appointed M&A adviser as the licensed party and take EGP's fee as a sub-advisory/referral fee from them or from the investor. Local counsel: yes, required. The desk is not a lawyer.

**Risks.**
- Timing: Insolvenzgeld lapses end-Nov 2026; opening ~1 Dec; plan by ~18 Dec. If no investor is credible by then the case drifts to an asset sale run by the appointed adviser or to liquidation of the German site — EGP's window is weeks, not months.
- Fee: the estate is cash-constrained; a EUR 25–35k fixed fee needs Sachwalter/committee approval and competes with the M&A adviser's own fee. Realistic chance that only a success fee is accepted, or that the fee has to be paid by the investor side.
- Role collision: a licensed M&A adviser is about to be appointed; EGP is a late entrant with no German mandate history. Mucha may decline anything that looks like a second sell-side.
- Owners unnamed; holding Julius Schüle GmbH outside the proceedings; no public debt/liquidity figures — size of the hole is a guess.
- Sector: auto die-casting overcapacity; AE Group precedent of a failed/long investor search; three-quarters of revenue is automotive.
- No fraud indicators found. Not a liquidation yet.

**Proposed fee.** Readiness EUR 25k on signature (English IM + group data-room supplement for non-EU investors; low end because the estate pays and the M&A adviser will do the German-language pack). Engagement EUR 25k. Success 5% of capital raised from EGP-introduced investors (not 7% — a court-supervised process with a mandated adviser will not bear more). Equity/warrants 1% of the new holdco (ask; expect to lose it). Potential fee: (EUR 50k ≈ USD 58k at an assumed EUR/USD 1.16) + 5% × midpoint EUR 27.5M (≈ USD 31.9M) = USD 1.595M → total ≈ USD 1.65M.
